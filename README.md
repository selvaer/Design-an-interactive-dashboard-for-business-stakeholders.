# Design-an-interactive-dashboard-for-business-stakeholders.

**📊 Sales Data Interactive Dashboard (Power BI)**

**🎯 Objective:**
**The goal of this project is to design an interactive sales dashboard using Power BI to help business stakeholders analyze key sales performance metrics, trends, and geographic insights effectively.**

🧰 Tools Used:
Power BI Desktop

**Sample Sales Dataset**

** Key Features & Visuals**
**1. KPI Card (Top Left) – Total Sales**
Displays the total sum of sales (₹10.03M).
Gives stakeholders a quick snapshot of revenue performance.

**2. Goal Card with Area Chart – Sum of PRICEEACH and SALES by STATE**
Shows the combined price per item and sales by different states.
Uses a KPI goal tracking visual to highlight performance (currently -97.54% from the goal).

**3. Time Series Analysis (Top Right)**
Visualizes the trend of product price and order status count against total sales.
Helps in understanding product value changes and order processing behavior over time.

**4. Map Visualization – Sales by State and Country**
Uses a map visual to show sales distribution across global locations.
Allows stakeholders to quickly identify regional performance and market coverage.

**5. Slicer – Quantity Ordered**
An interactive slider to filter sales data based on the quantity of items ordered.
Enhances user interaction and customized exploration.

**6. Status Filters (Checklist)***
Enables filtering the report based on order status like Shipped, Resolved, On Hold, etc.

Useful for identifying sales performance by operational status.

**📈 Insights & Business Use:**


**This dashboard supports:**
Monitoring sales performance across time and region.
Identifying underperforming states or countries.
Tracking product pricing and sales correlation.
Helping managers take quick actions based on live, filtered data
